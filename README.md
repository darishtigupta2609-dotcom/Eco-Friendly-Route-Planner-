📌 About the Project

EcoRoute is an environmental awareness project developed as part of our Environmental Studies coursework.

The project focuses on promoting sustainable transportation by helping users understand the environmental impact of different travel choices.

It aims to create awareness about fuel consumption, carbon emissions, and the importance of choosing environmentally responsible transportation options.

🎯 Objectives
Promote awareness about transportation-related pollution.
Encourage sustainable and eco-friendly travel choices.
Help users compare different transportation options.
Highlight the importance of reducing carbon emissions.
Encourage responsible travelling habits.
✨ Key Features
Route Comparison: Explore different travel options.
Distance Analysis: Understand the distance associated with journeys.
Fuel Consumption: Explore estimated fuel usage.
Carbon Emission Awareness: Understand the estimated environmental impact of transportation.
User-Friendly Interface: Simple and accessible design.
