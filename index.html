Environmental Impact

The way we travel affects the air we breathe and the climate we live in.

Carbon Footprint

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases, such as carbon dioxide (CO₂), released by an activity. Burning petrol or diesel in vehicles is a major source of transportation-related CO₂.

Air Pollution

Vehicle exhaust can add harmful pollutants to the air, especially in busy cities. Choosing cleaner ways to travel can help improve local air quality.

Fuel Consumption

The more fuel a vehicle burns, the more CO₂ it produces. Shorter trips, shared rides and public transport can reduce the fuel used per person.

Sustainable Transportation

Walking, cycling, public transport, carpooling and electric vehicles are all ways to lower the environmental cost of getting from one place to another.

About the Project

Problem

Many people choose how to travel without knowing how much fuel they use or how much carbon dioxide their trip produces.

Solution

EcoRoute is a web-based prototype that compares travel options using distance, estimated fuel consumption and estimated carbon emissions, and highlights the lower-emission choice.

Technology Used

  • HTML: page structure
  • CSS: design, layout and responsive appearance
  • JavaScript: calculations, comparisons and dynamic results

Assumptions

  • Distances come from a small set of sample routes stored in the project.
  • Fuel used = Distance ÷ Vehicle mileage.
  • CO₂ = Fuel used × selected emission factor.
  • Bus emissions use an estimated CO₂-per-passenger-kilometre value.
  • Bicycle and walking produce no CO₂ during travel.
